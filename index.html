📱 Add Food Hub App to Android Home Screen

You can install this app directly onto your Android Home Screen with the yellow Food hub icon—no Play Store required:

  1. Open this file in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (3-dot menu) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. The Food hub launcher icon will appear on your home screen and open full-screen offline.

🖨️ 58mm / 80mm Bluetooth Thermal Printer Guide

  • Direct Bluetooth (BLE): Turn on your thermal printer (MTP-II, PT-210, Retsol, TVSE, Xprinter) and tap Connect Printer at the top.
  • Android RawBT Print: If your printer uses Classic Bluetooth (PIN 0000 or 1234), install the free RawBT app on Android, pair your printer once, and tap Print via RawBT on any bill.
  • System Print: Tap System 58mm Print to print through Android's built-in print service.
